THE LEGEND OF THE GOLDEN LLAMA RULES

SYMBOLS & PAYLINES:

Pays left to right across 5 lines. Symbols include Andean Flutes (🪈), Ponchos (🧣), Sun Tablets (☀️), Golden Idols (🗿), and Fluffy Alpacas (🦙).

GOLDEN LLAMA SCATTERS (✨🦙✨):

Substitutes as Wild and feeds coins into the 3 Pack-Mule Llamas on the side.

THE 3 PACK-MULE POT BONUSES:

Each Golden Llama has a chance to burst open one or more of the pack bags:

  • Extra Wilds Llama: Awards 8 Free Spins with Inca Cactus Wilds (🌵) appearing on the middle reels.
  • Jackpots Llama: Awards 8 Free Spins where Golden Llamas drop Mini, Minor, Major, or Grand Jackpot coins!
  • Multiplier Llama: Awards 8 Free Spins with an active 2X, 3X, or 5X global multiplier on all payouts.
  • Combo Super Bonus: If 2 or 3 packs burst at the same time, all their perks combine!

FREE SPIN RETRIGGERS:

During Free Spins, landing 2 or more natural Golden Llamas on a spin awards +3 Additional Free Spins (up to 3 retriggers maximum).
